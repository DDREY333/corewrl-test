# Coreworld IV — Threat Log for Moderator Review
**Campaign:** Coreworld IV (format: The End of Nothing; preset: Burnplan / Medium start)
**Player character:** Enn (Rigger)
**Scope:** Drop 1 (GT-9) and Drop 2 (CR-11, urgent/red)
**Contains narrator-side spoilers. Not for the player.**

---

## 0. Caveats on sources

- **GT-9** was narrated in an earlier session. This narrator has only Campaign Ledger #1 for it (the player-facing record). The narrator's original hidden design for GT-9 isn't available. GT-9 entries below are reconstructed from what was shown on the page, with interpretation marked as such.

## 2. GT-9: Old grain terminal, river flats (Drop 1)

**Handler:** Dorsey (human)
**Crew:** Enn (Rigger, rookie), Sol Teague (Driller, 4th drop)
**Quota:** 4 intact crystals
**Pay:** 30/head on quota, +3 per intact past quota. Regraded mid-drop to 45/head and +5 per intact past.
**Window:** opened 6h after touchdown, held 40 min.
**Outcome:** 4/4 quota. Both survived. Sol injured: forearm laceration (11 stitches), boot toecap removed by the Hisser's light. Drill #14 lost its bit and chuck. Pod hatch damaged and rebuilt in the field.

### Threats, as observed and reconstructed

1. **Glass crawler** (cultist creation)
   - **Behavior on page:** Rang in sympathy with crystal hum and with struck rebar. Went for the crystal, not for people. Plates starred and legs snapped under fire. A dark core with a red spark; shooting the core killed it. Crumbled to pale flakes matching the crust deposits found around the site.
   - **Signs before it appeared:** Glittering flake smear on a hopper car. Pale crust under the drip and the crack. A thin ring from high in silo 3 during drill pauses.
   - **Result:** Killed by Enn (4 hits, last 2 to the core). Cut Sol's forearm.
2. **"The Glassman"** (cultist practitioner; the player's name for him)
   - **Behavior on page:** Watched from the weigh-house, then walked to the pod and got inside it. Tied the hatch wheel shut with a seat strap. Tampered with the beacon. Talked, and tried to bargain for the red. Hid under the porthole from the Ticker's light.
   - **Craft:** Grows or "tunes" glass creatures with a song or crystal resonance. Five chest lines with short cuts between them.
   - **Result:** Shot in the side from 35 to 40 m during a volley. Later killed by Sol's head shot through the hinge gap after the crew drilled out the hinge pins. His body was left by the pod; whether it was collected is unconfirmed.
3. **Ticker** (ACC scanner drone)
   - **Behavior:** Identical tick ("fingernail along a comb"). Straight lines at walking pace. Spinning slate-grey shape with a sweeping flat light, blind directly beneath itself. Narrowed its light to a point on the hatch hinge corner, leaving a clean steel coin (a mark).
4. **Throb** (ACC carrier)
   - **Behavior:** Deep chest-felt pulses. Brought the Hisser and later left.
5. **Hisser** (ACC collector)
   - **Behavior:** Sound like poured sand. A flat matte sheet with a white light line along its front edge. Erased bodies, rubble, tape, boot leather, and drill steel, leaving mirror-clean ground. Spared walls and the sealed pod hull.
   - **Result:** Took Sol's toecap, the foil tape, and the drill's bit and chuck through the hinge seam.

**Why the rating was likely too high (narrator's reconstruction):**
- The crew of 2 faced it, which raises real danger. But the clearly lethal threats (the ACC collector, the practitioner) were mostly avoidable by staying sealed and under concrete.
- The one fight (the crawler) was won cleanly.
- The worst moment was a puzzle-like lock-in (cultist inside the pod) that the crew solved with tools and time rather than a costly fight.

## 3. CR-11: Refinery complex, coastal plain (Drop 2, urgent/red)

**Handler:** Cassel (AI)
**Crew:** Enn (Rigger, 2nd drop), Desmond Holt (Hauler, 3rd drop)
**Quota:** 3 intact crystals
**Pay:** 40/head on quota, +6 per intact past quota. Regraded mid-drop to 55/head and +8 per intact past.
**Window:** opened 5h after touchdown, held 30 min.
**Briefing:** reduced. Smoke cover; filters "adequate for some hours"; crystals at the base of cracking tower 3 and the east end of the tank farm; "another pod on site".
**Outcome:** Quota exceeded: 8 intact (5 from the crew, 3 from pod two's satchel), plus 4 cracked and about 13 chips. Both crew survived with no wounds. A stranded miner from pod two (Varga) was rescued on the pod floor. Net pay for Enn was 83 Caps.

### Design intent (at the time)

- **Target actual difficulty:** about 8 (a red slip: crew of 2, harder than GT-9).
- **Site rules:**
  - Burning oil fires everywhere.
  - Smoke can false-trip tripwire beams (told to the player by the handler beforehand).
  - Cold, clean air near the sea on the east end.
  - A new batch of filters (rims numbered 40 and up) lacks the charcoal layer, so it fails in smoke. This was set up on the ship and shown on the drop by pod two's brown #43 filter left on the steps.
  - Tower 2 is structurally failing (the periodic "bong").
- **Intended turn:** The pall's need for heat drives it to the warm grey box in the pod. It drapes over the crew's own pod and freezes the cell and the beacon. The crew's safe place becomes the problem.
- **Intended counters:**
  - Bait the pall with flares or fire.
  - Use a cultist coat (made of pall skin, which hides the wearer's heat).
  - Revive the cell with Rigger field repair.
  - Hold out sealed.

### Threats

1. **The pall** (beast; miners' name; also used by the handler, who called it a name "partners" had given it)
   - **True nature:** A huge, thin, translucent sheet that rides smoke updrafts and feeds on heat. It comes down on the strongest nearby heat source and stays until that source is cold, draining it and leaving a frost ring. It ignores sound. It doesn't notice living people while stronger heat is near or while their heat is masked, but it will take a person if they're the best heat around. Cultists wear coats made from its skin, which hide their heat. A punctured or dead wearer leaks heat (visible as steam threads) and draws it.
   - **Lethality:** Complete when it happens (smothering and freezing). Rare.
   - **Signs given before it mattered:**
     - Burning pools went dark "as if a lid was put on".
     - A dark "sag" in the smoke over the oil lake.
     - Holt: "The fires keep going out."
     - Varga's account: "Comes down on a fire and sits on it till it's out… never came down on us. Only fire… When he went down, the thing came down."
     - Cassel's reports: flare, low fires, lone partner.
     - The pod's box was noted as warm at touchdown.
   - **On page:**
     - Took two coat-man corpses (frost rings).
     - Drawn off the crystal tank by a thrown flare, and stayed on it. A road flare can't be smothered.
     - Smothered a wounded, living coat man after the Explosive tore his coat. This is the one time it killed on screen.
     - Draped over pod 4 as the planned turn, freezing the hatch wheel and draining the cell.
     - Drawn off the pod by steaming bodies in the pump house, then by a second flare plus burning tar.
     - Possibly removed by the ACC survey team: a long "tearing canvas" sound twice, the second time near the flare. Never confirmed to the player.
   - **Execution problems:**
     - Two flares solved it every time. It never threatened a living crew member directly.
     - The turn (the pall on the pod) was undone by the crew's own fight, before the window.
     - The "frost cracks crystals" consequence landed only lightly (one crew crystal and one of pod two's).
2. **Coat men** (cultists; no name or technique given on page)
   - **Intended:** People unafraid of the pall, walking under it "like weather" and wearing its skin. They carry long bundles wrapped in pall skin: captured miners or bodies, including pod two's driller Addo, whose boots were visible. Armed with sharpened hooked ribs. They signal with two-note breathy whistles (low, then high) and answer each other. They come looking when their people go quiet. They lift their coats over their heads whenever the wind clears the smoke: a planned pre-fight reaction, shown twice, left unexplained.
   - **On page:**
     - Two walked straight down an open strip at the crew. One split off to flank. Both died: one shot at 30 m (3 hits, then 1 into the lump), the other killed at point-blank range after his flank, which nearly hit Holt and cut the drill sling.
     - Three came to investigate the corpse. One crouched and triggered the Explosive tripwire; one was thrown; one was smothered by the pall.
     - Two at the pump house: one cut through the tin roof with the rib and looked down through the gash; the other rushed the door. Both were killed by pistol fire.
   - **Execution problems (per the player and agreed):** The rules call for cover, flanking, pulling back, bait, hostages, and striking through walls. What appeared was mostly approaching in the open and charging armed miners with melee weapons. The roof cut was the only real "strike through walls" move, and it was followed by a frontal rush. They never used the pall, the smoke, the bundles, Varga as bait, or the crew's predictable return to the pod against them. They never used ranged attacks or traps.
3. **"The scraper"** (unseen; inside tower 3)
   - **Intended:** A slow, regular blade-on-hide scraping heard from the tower doorway; the practitioner who makes the coats. A signature sound before sight. Didn't respond to the drill, the gunfire, or the explosion. The player read that correctly and left ("oh hell no").
   - **On page:** Never encountered. A loose thread, along with the bundles and Addo's fate.
4. **Comb / Ticker** (ACC scanner; Holt's word "comb", Enn's "Ticker")
   - **On page:** Approached after the Explosive. Passed over the crew, who hid under a pipe bridge in its blind spot directly below. Marked the blast site with a point of light. The Throb and Hisser then collected there and left. Later scanned the bodies of the fallen.
   - **Threat to the crew:** Indirect only.
5. **Survey team** (ACC, escalation tier 2; first appearance)
   - **Intended:** A tell of perfectly synchronized steps ("one heavy person walking", 6 to 8 of them), told to the player in advance by Cassel. Thorough and indifferent. Goes to where the noise was.
   - **On page:** Checked the pump house with a hum. Walked to the pod. Ran a blue scanning line round the entire hatch seam and a glow on the porthole. Then left toward the flare and burning tar (the possible pall removal).
   - **Threat to the crew:** Psychological only. It never forced a decision beyond "stay still and dark".
6. **Environment**
   - **Smoke / filters:** Pod two's paper filters went brown in about 4 hours. Enn and Holt both carried old-batch charcoal filters, partly through their own ship-side investigation (the corner veterans, Okafor). Varga, unmasked for hours, survived thanks to cleaner sea wind. Not much pressure on the crew.
   - **Tower 2:** Leaning and bonging. Dropped a grating platform 15 m from the crew. Collapsed off-screen while the crew waited in the pod.
   - **Cold:** The pall froze the pod. Varga became hypothermic and the crew gave up their jackets. Enn briefly lost consciousness on the ascent (thin air, 3 people in a 2-seat pod). The cell weakened, and the beacon flickered until Enn's repair.
   - **Overturned tanker:** A large clean crystal under an unstable frame. The player declined it; no consequence.
7. **Pod two** (another crew on site; Dorsey's, white slip)
   - **Fate:** Driller Addo was captured or killed and wrapped as a bundle at tower 3. Scout Varga had a broken leg, was hypothermic, and tapped a pistol on a rail for hours. The crew splinted him, left him in the pump house to do quota, then took him home in exchange for his satchel (4 crystals, a flare, water). Pod two's window closed while he waited.
   - **Note:** This is where most of the drop's real cost fell, on NPCs rather than the player's crew.

### Why CR-11 played easier than intended (narrator self-assessment)

1. The cultists were tactically passive or suicidal (see the player's comment).
2. The pall had one clear, cheap counter (flares), and the crew had two flares plus a third heat source.
3. The ACC escalated (comb, then carrier, then survey team) but never forced a costly choice.
4. The planned turn (the pall on the pod) was resolved by the crew's own fight before it cost them anything.
5. The crew suffered no injuries. Costs were Caps (uplinks, fees), gear (all modules spent, flares, tape, splint, antiseptic, a drill sling), hearing and nose damage, and exposure. All were recoverable.
6. Pay ended well above quota (83 net). The prompt warns that drops running "too smooth" should feel suspicious and shouldn't last. This was the second clean drop in a row: two quotas, two regrades, no crew losses. In-world, that is being flagged: the admin/Allocation summons is on the pay slip, and Sol said "Twice is when they start reading you."

### The player's good play (it was real and should count)
- Recognized that the scraper ignored noise and walked away.
- An Explosive tripwire on a corpse as bait for investigators.
- Baited the pall with flares, twice, and placed them deliberately.
- Repaired the cell: cleaned the terminals, pulled fuses for non-essential draws, insulated it.
- Bolted the hatch and went dark when the survey team approached.
- Kept Varga conscious by having him count down by sevens.
- Used the ship-side intel (filters, Pitch, drone tells) on the drop.
