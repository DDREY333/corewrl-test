## 3. Play rules in force (on top of the main prompt)
- **Nightmares:**
  - Don't announce them. Slip in mid-scene.
  - Make them long and expansive, with dream logic, and personal rather than a replay of the drop.
  - Horror first. The uncanny must serve dread, never whimsy.
  - Give Enn something they want in the dream and deny it.
  - The threat has its own rules and comes closer every beat. Slow the prose as it nears. Keep Enn vulnerable. Stay with the threat; don't tour locations.
  - Callbacks to waking life should unsettle, not wink.
  - Use irregular spacing with plain line breaks and blank lines only, never leading spaces or code blocks. Let it widen as the dream worsens.
  - Absorb real body sensations. Keep the threat at the edges.
  - No stock beats: sound coming from you, pressing from below, falling, teeth, waking with a gasp or clenched jaw.
  - Never explain the dream.
  - At meaningful choices inside a dream, STOP and let the player decide. Some dreams are lucid, some are linear; never say which.
  - End mid-sentence, on a fragment, or with a real sound replacing the dream. Never a jolt awake.
  - Nightmares are uncommon. They never create threats in the world.
- **Injuries:** bold an injury the moment it happens, whether it's Enn's or the crew's. Never bold hidden clues.
- **Group orders:** an order from Enn to the group includes Enn unless stated otherwise.
- **Noise ladder:** each level is roughly twice as loud as the one below. Duration matters. Noise feeds the heat clock.
  - **1:** whispering, hard breathing, creaking gear. Carries a few metres.
  - **2:** talking, careful walking on debris. Tens of metres.
  - **3:** running, dragging a loaded satchel, cranking a radio, prying a panel by hand. A few dozen metres.
  - **4:** hand-chipping crystal, shouting, metal on metal. About 100 m.
  - **5:** the drill on a sound bit. Several hundred metres, sustained.
  - **6:** the drill on dense crystal or a worn bit, forcing a hatch with bar and sledge, a pod leg or hull tearing, a pod's braking burn (seconds only). Half a kilometre or more.
  - **7:** one shot from the Shooter's rifle. 1–2 km; a sharp crack that's easy to trace.
  - **8:** one shot from the backup pistol, or a pod's launch burn. Several kilometres.
  - **9:** a breaching charge.
  - **10:** a demolition charge.
  - **−1/−2:** too faint for the unaided ear.
- **Noise readouts:**
  - When Enn makes noise at 4 or higher, or the player asks, end the reply with a readout, e.g. `Noise: ||||?····· (4–6/10)`.
  - Enn is UNTRAINED, so readouts are a range about three levels wide. The range narrows as Enn keeps paying attention to sound.
  - Signaller headphones read exactly from −2 to 7. Anything above 7 is clipped into static and briefly deafens.
  - Never show the heat clock.
- **Everything else:** the main prompt's rules apply (Prep Actions, Check Attributes, Pass the Time, OOC conventions, ledgers).

## 13. Drop summaries
**Drop 1: RS-4.** Rotation 1. Handler Dorrit. Pod 14.
- **Launch:** the flip board counted down in minutes: LAUNCH — 06, 05, 03, 02, 01. Enn never saw a clock time.
- **Descent:** length unknown to Enn. It "felt long."
- **Counter answer:** none given. No counter or question was posed at return; the bay clerk just counted: *"Six. Plus pieces."*
- **Terms:** quota 6 intact. 30 Caps each on quota; +4 per crystal over quota, crew-divided; −5 each per crystal short. The window opens 5 hours after touchdown and lasts 30 minutes.
- **Terrain:** a drained reservoir bed. White cracked crust is safe; grey or dark seams are wet and deep. Terraces step down to a grey drowned town with a tower stump. A dam to the north, its crest broken in two places. A rubble ridge (the old shore) to the east. Late afternoon, overcast, steady west wind, burnt smell.
- **Landing:** the pod landed tilted on a raised white slab, with one leg sunk into grey.
- **Signs and events, in order:**
  1. **Post.** An ACC post about 300 m east on the ridge. White, head height, seamless, with a turning band and a dark slit. Bare, dust-free ground around its base. No chime heard.
  2. **Drag groove.** Body-width, coming from the dam into the town, with star-shaped cracks in the crust. No footprints.
  3. **The split.** Enn and Dace went west through the open; Tam and Hollis went through the town.
  4. **Bodies on the crest.** Four bodies pinned upright on twisted bone stakes, facing down over the bed. A fifth shape was a bent railing. One had a flapping sleeve; one had its jaw open.
  5. **Crystals at the dam foot.** Grown from rust-brown stains. They hum when drilled. Tam cut rings into the concrete.
  6. **Heap thumps.** While the crew waited by the dam, thumps came from the tower.
  7. **Heap's first response.** The first drilling stopped the thumps. The second drilling brought the heap out of the tower, straight up the main street toward the noise: about 9 heaves per crystal, 3–4 m per heave. It froze whenever the drill stopped.
  8. **Heap turns.** On the third crystal, it turned off the street. It now followed footprints without needing the drill.
  9. **Retreat.** The crew left the fan through the town. They found the tower nest: a floor of clear crystals around a bare, cart-sized oval, with loose bone strips and rags. Tam hand-pulled #4, a cracked one, and #5. Her boots were soaked in muck.
  10. **Muck trail.** The heap followed Tam's muck prints and sped up on them, to about double speed or more.
  11. **Decoy.** Dace dragged Tam's boots and threw them into a deep grey patch to the WSW. The heap sank and stuck there. Its underside is many flat, grey-pink hands.
  12. **Shoring.** The pod's sunk leg kept going down. Hollis shored it with 5–6 stacked plates.
  13. **Sixth crystal.** Enn and Dace went back to the nest. Enn reached in from the sill and pulled #6 by hand: one "tink," a change of angle, then clean. Enn left the glove there.
  14. **Post locks on.** Seen through the scope from inside the town on Enn and Dace's second trip: the post's band had stopped turning, its slit fixed on the pod slab. It's Dace's *theory*, not confirmed, that the cause was Hollis's plate-cracking and the hatch latch banging twice.
  15. **White bar.** A white bar drone came over the ridge, hovered over the post, aligned with the slit, and flew the line to the pod. It made a cross over all four crew. Uplink to Dorrit.
  16. **Leveller.** A white leveller, house-row wide, came over the ridge. It planed the ground smooth, flowed around the post, aligned with the line, and came for the slab.
  17. **Lift.** The pod lifted at 5:00 by the dial. The leg tore off. **Hollis: head wound.** **Dace: forearm broken.** The rifle hit the hull and the scope lens cracked. Through the porthole: the slab planed smooth, the heap gone, *three* upright figures on the crest.
- **Yield:** 6 intact (III, III, II, I, I, I), plus 1 cracked crystal in 2 pieces.
- **Grading:** met quota. Net 21 each.
- **Aftermath:** Pod 14 is on HOLD. Two faint, clean lines cross over its hatch, nose to tail and side to side. They won't buff off or scratch off.
