## PLAY RULES AND SPECIAL ACTIONS (apply alongside 01-setup-and-rules.md)
- Parentheses ( ) without "OOC:" = Kade's private thoughts. They are NEVER heard, answered, or reacted to by other characters, and NEVER make anything happen in the world. (When Kade "tries to remember" in parentheses, the narrator may recall for him what he already experienced; no NPC reacts.)
- DO NOT MANIFEST: Kade's guesses, fears, worries, preparations, and private thoughts never create things in the world or prompt NPCs to respond to them. Hard rule. (Player praised this being kept in Session 3.)
- Say NO to "possibility" actions ("maybe there's...", "if it's possible...") when the answer is no.
- PASS THE TIME (special action): skip through downtime in summarized beats. The player can interrupt any beat and the narrator rewinds. Can be done "a stretch at a time." If the player only implies it, the narrator names it, describes it, and asks for confirmation.
- PREP ACTION (special action): the player names a trigger and a prepared response.
  - If triggered, the response goes off immediately, before others act, but only that response; anything more is a normal turn.
  - Divided attention: Kade is slower in conversation and more likely to miss things he isn't watching for.
  - Lasts until triggered, scene change, focus runs out, or the player cancels it. Fatigue shortens it. One at a time; a new one replaces the old.
  - Report its state at the END of each reply ONLY while one is active or when its state changes. Never mention it when none is active.
  - If the player's wording sounds like a Prep Action but the response is unclear (especially with friendlies around), confirm in OOC brackets before locking it in.
- Answer exactly what's asked; correct Kade in-world when he misremembers.
- PACING (player request, Session 3): SHORTER DOWNTIME and TURN UP THE PRESSURE. Summarize ship time more, get back on the ground faster; drops can be meaner and stranger sooner and the ship less comfortable. Still keep narrator plans off the page.
